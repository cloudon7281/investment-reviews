# Research Charter C — Comparative Investment Research

**Status:** Standard charter, accompanying *Investment Research Programme Brief* v1.0  
**Version:** v1.0 · 14 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Produce a holdings-neutral ranking of prospective investment merit and, when used for a portfolio review, separately derive **portfolio-aware** recommendations.

## 1. Invocation, inputs and universe

Use for **both monthly and annual reviews** of thematic beta-plus and alpha-seeking investments, and for substantial ad hoc comparative investigations. The default universe comprises **all currently recorded `candidate` ideas plus current holdings in the review's agreed scope**. In-scope holdings remain covered regardless of their prior analysis verdict; admit additional discoveries only through the approved scope-change process.

**Monthly:** consume the completed Charter B promotion report, latest candidate ResearchBriefs/analyses, the Strategy/theses, and the authoritative `investment-reviews` **Periodic Review** plus previous decisions/proof points. Normal monthly direct-stock scope is ISA/taxable; thematic beta-plus opportunities are compared on prospective merit, without imposing the full annual fund-compliance exercise or silently expanding pension holdings review.

**Annual:** same opportunity-research method, plus the Operations Brief's broader consolidated performance, asset allocation, pension, cash/tax and broad-market beta/fund review. The broad-market beta fund compliance/benchmark task is **not** this charter or an `investment-analysis` responsibility.

**Ad hoc:** state the exact universe and comparative question; no Periodic Review required unless an actual holdings review/portfolio recommendation is requested.

## 2. Two deliberately separated streams

**Stream 1 — Investment merit, holdings-neutral.** Treat a held security and a newly discovered one as equally eligible. Researchers use business, price, valuation, risks, credible shareholder outcomes and comparisons with peers and passive alternatives; they do **not** score a stock for purchase price, unrealised P&L, position weight or incumbent status. All cases receive a current judgement, though previously verified dossiers may be refreshed rather than rewritten from scratch.

**Stream 2 — Holdings governance, holdings-aware.** Run in parallel under the programme lead using actual `investment-reviews` outputs: previous `New`, `Increased`, `Retained`, `Sold` decision outcomes; current positions, path-to-doubling/profit-taking checkpoints; absolute 90-day-high stop-loss review/exit triggers and smoothed/P90 context; volatility and persistence; moonshot proof windows; previous exceptions, dates, pending trades and concentration. Verify strategy triggers without confusing last-period P&L with lifetime return or the `Sold` counterfactual with actual performance. **Every mandatory holding trigger must reach a documented resolution/escalation**, independently of ranking.

**Integration:** after the independent ranking, apply actual position sizes, wrappers, taxes, liquidity, correlations, investment constraints and switching costs. State separately investment merit and whether to buy, add, retain, reduce, rotate or take no action. A better-ranking investment does not mechanically force rotation when switching cost offsets the benefit.

## 3. Research procedure and parallel assignments

1. **Normalise:** reconcile candidate/holding coverage and genuine issuer/security IDs; preserve multiple distinct idea histories; group by economically meaningful peer set and common factor.
2. **Share research:** prepare reusable sector context, then a substantive company/ETF dossier per case with primary evidence, valuation/share basis, scenarios where supportable, alternatives, specific falsifiers and dated proof points.
3. **Challenge:** separate researchers test the assumptions, adverse cases, strongest rival instruments and alternate technologies. Record objections and disposition, not merely a confidence number.
4. **Compare within sectors:** use consistent assumptions and explicit pairwise choices, with ties and incomparability where evidence is limited.
5. **Calibrate across sectors:** produce **all-universe** holdings-neutral tiers/ranking; identify factor overlap and leading alternatives against broad-market and relevant thematic-beta options. Shortlist only for intensive *additional* research where it can change choices; no predetermined winner quota.
6. **Discover:** at sector and cross-sector checkpoints, log stronger competitors or adjacent opportunities. A comparator is not automatically an official idea; recommend admission to the user where justified; use the Operations Brief `IdeaSubmission` approval/ingestion path.
7. **Focus and integrate:** deepen the highest-value unresolved questions, then combine with holdings governance to prepare practical allocation proposals. Retain conditional positions only with appropriately dated proof; do not assume buying is required to fill a stock allocation budget.

## 4. Gates and outputs

**Coverage gate:** every in-scope candidate and holding is researched/reassessed or visibly blocked. No winner-only reporting.  
**Challenge gate:** every material leading case receives a genuine independent challenge and strongest-alternative comparison.  
**Calibration gate:** holdings-neutral all-universe ranking/tiering is explainable and not the average of incompatible subagent scores.  
**Portfolio gate (reviews only):** the Periodic/Annual Review outcome discussion and **all mandatory holdings triggers** are addressed; actual allocation proposals are separate from ranking.  
**Record gate:** only concluded new research judgements produce official, dated, superseding `AnalysisRecord`s; no trade YAML before explicit agreement.

Produce: (a) full issuer/idea research register; (b) shared sector notes and individual dossiers; (c) independent challenges and peer rankings; (d) all-universe comparative opportunity ranking with research-confidence/gap fields; (e) Discovery Register and admission proposals; (f) a **separate** holdings-trigger closure sheet and portfolio-aware allocation recommendations when applicable; (g) decision-ready deep dossiers for leading cases; (h) appropriate schema-validated research records and import manifests; (i) the monthly/annual discussion outputs required by the *Investment Operations Brief*, including holds and no-action conclusions.

## 5. Working principles and exceptions

Investment criteria are exclusively those of the current Strategy; no separate numeric score or hidden return hurdle. Initial candidate or holding status does not pre-ordain the result. Source disagreements, currency/share-count conflicts, stale filings and speculative probabilities must remain visible. Use a common price/FX cut-off where possible, distinguish sector-specific valuation methods, and avoid confusing thematic enthusiasm with company-specific shareholder alpha. Portfolio-aware output can disagree with a strict merits rank—**explain why**.

**Run-manifest minimum:** charter C/version, review type (monthly/annual/ad hoc), candidate snapshot, holdings scope and authoritative report versions, dated metrics/cut-off, baseline analyses, exclusions, permissions, research-package plan, agreed gates and scope amendments.
